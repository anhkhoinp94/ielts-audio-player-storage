Talk about an activity you usually do that wastes time.

I want to talk about an activity I usually do that wastes a lot of my time, which is scrolling on TikTok. I tend to use it whenever I have free time, especially in the evening before going to bed.

The information I get from TikTok is often shallow and not very useful, but somehow it is extremely addictive. I often get pulled into scrolling for hours without realizing it, which leads to going to bed late or having trouble sleeping. As a result, I frequently lack sleep and feel tired and unfocused during work and study time.

This habit has clearly had a negative impact on my daily life. I have thought about deleting TikTok many times, but honestly, it’s very hard to completely quit, especially when I have nothing else to do.

For now, the method I’m trying is letting my phone run out of battery and charging it at night. That way, I remove the chance to use my phone before sleeping. I hope this small change will help me manage my time better and improve my sleep quality.
