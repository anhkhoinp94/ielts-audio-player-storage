Describe something you were given that was very useful.

I would like to describe something I was given that turned out to be very useful, and that is a handheld vacuum cleaner. I received it as a Christmas gift from one of my friends, and at first, I didn’t expect it to make such a big difference in my daily life.

My house is located near a main road, so it gets dusty very quickly. No matter how often I swept or wiped the floor, it never felt truly clean. This vacuum cleaner completely changed that. I now use it regularly to clean my house, especially areas where dust easily accumulates.

Thanks to this device, my house has become much cleaner than before, and the air feels fresher as well. What’s more, it has made me enjoy cleaning more, instead of seeing it as a tiring chore.

Beyond its practical value, this gift shows my friend’s thoughtfulness, because it perfectly fits my living situation. For me, this vacuum cleaner is not just a useful tool, but also something that improves my comfort and quality of life every day.
