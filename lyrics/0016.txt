Describe a piece of furniture in your house.

I would like to describe a piece of furniture in my house that I really like, which is a wooden table and chair set in the living room. This set is placed right in the center of the living room, where my family usually welcomes guests.

It is made of wood and is beautifully carved with detailed patterns, which makes it look traditional and elegant. The set includes two small chairs for one person each, one large chair that can seat three people, and a wooden table in the middle. 

We mainly use it to receive guests, chat, and enjoy tea together. Sometimes, it is also used as a dining table for family meals.

This piece of furniture is important to me because it is where my family spends time together and where guests feel welcomed when they visit our home. It creates a warm and friendly atmosphere in the house. For me, this wooden furniture set is not just practical, but also represents comfort, tradition, and family connection.
