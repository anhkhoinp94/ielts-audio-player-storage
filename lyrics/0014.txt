Describe an item you bought but rarely use.

I would like to describe an item I bought but rarely use, which is an open-face helmet. I bought it because I thought it would be perfect for traveling and it also matched my new manual motorbike very well.

At that time, I imagined myself going on long trips and enjoying the experience of riding freely. However, in reality, I hardly use this helmet. The main reason is that it takes more time to put on because it fits quite tightly, while my half helmet is much more convenient. I can just place it on my head and go.

Every time I see the open-face helmet, I feel a bit regretful. I have even made plans to travel far and use it, but I’ve been too busy to make those plans come true.

Still, I don’t want to sell it because I really like it. I just hope that in the future, when my work is better organized, I’ll have more chances to travel and finally use this helmet the way I originally intended.
