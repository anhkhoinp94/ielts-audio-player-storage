Talk about someone who has had a big impact on you.

I want to talk about someone who has had a big impact on me, and that person is my former team lead. I met him when I first joined the company, and he was the one who guided me through everything during my early days.

What I admire the most about him is that he taught me everything he knew without holding anything back. Even in situations where I understood something more clearly than he did, he never acted superior. Instead, he listened to me carefully, even though he was my leader.

Because of him, I learned the importance of listening and being open-minded, no matter how experienced I am or how little someone else may know. His attitude showed me that true leadership comes from humility and the willingness to learn from anyone.

Thanks to him, I now try to listen to people with more or less experience than me, and I always stay ready to share and support others whenever I can. He truly changed the way I work and the way I treat people around me.
