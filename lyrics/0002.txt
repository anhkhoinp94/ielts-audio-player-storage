What do you usually do in your free time?

I usually spend my free time either tidying up my house or playing sports, because these activities help me feel productive and active. One of my favorite hobbies is playing football, and I really enjoy the energy and teamwork involved in the game.

Whenever I feel stressed, I like to take a walk in the park, as it allows me to relax and clear my mind. Unlike many people, I’m not very interested in watching movies, listening to music, or reading books, because I prefer activities that keep me moving and physically engaged.

I also regularly participate in sports, and football is definitely my favorite. Playing sports not only keeps me healthy, but it also gives me a sense of enjoyment and achievement.

Overall, I think being active is an essential part of my lifestyle, and I always try to find time for physical activities, whether it’s playing football or simply walking outdoors.
