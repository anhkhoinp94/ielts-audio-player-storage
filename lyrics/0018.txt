Talk about a city or town you visited and liked a lot.

I would like to talk about a city I visited and liked a lot, which is the coastal city of Phan Thiet. I have been there many times, usually during the summer holidays.

What I love most about Phan Thiet is the sea and the fresh, clean air. The atmosphere there is always peaceful, which makes it a perfect place to relax and escape from the busy city life. Another thing I really enjoy is the seafood, because it is very fresh and delicious.

When I stay in Phan Thiet, I like swimming in the sea, enjoying seafood meals, and sometimes drinking beer with my family. One of my favorite activities is taking a walk along the beach in the evening, when the weather is cooler and the view is very calm and beautiful.

I always want to return to Phan Thiet every summer because it helps me recharge after a long period of hard work. More importantly, it gives me valuable time to rest and enjoy meaningful moments with my family.

Khi ở Phan Thiết, tôi thích bơi biển, thưởng thức các bữa ăn hải sản, và đôi khi uống bia cùng gia đình. Một trong những hoạt động yêu thích của tôi là đi dạo dọc bờ biển vào buổi tối, khi thời tiết mát mẻ hơn và khung cảnh rất yên tĩnh, đẹp đẽ.
