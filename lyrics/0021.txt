Describe something you did to study another language.

I want to talk about something I do to study another language, which is English. Every day, I listen to English videos, learn new vocabulary, and practice speaking by myself.

In addition, because I am a programmer, I have developed my own applications to make listening to English audio easier and to help me study vocabulary more efficiently. I started learning English when I was a child, and my main goal is to go abroad for further studies and to work in an international environment.

English has never been my strongest subject, so my progress has always been quite slow. The only thing I can do is continue learning and find methods that suit me best.

Using the applications I created makes my learning more personalized, and I can focus on the areas I need to improve. Thanks to these methods, I feel that I am gradually making progress. Although it takes a lot of time and effort, I believe that persistence and the right approach will help me achieve my language goals in the future.
