Talk about a piece of technology you often rely on.

I would like to talk about a piece of technology I often rely on, and that is my laptop. I’ve been using it regularly since I started university.

At first, it was mainly used for studying, doing assignments, reading news, and some entertainment. However, now it has become much more important to me because I use it for work. I am a programmer, so my laptop is my main working tool and also the way I earn a living.

I use it every day to write code, solve problems, and complete my tasks. Without it, I simply wouldn’t be able to do my job properly. Honestly, I haven’t really imagined what my life would be like without my laptop, but I believe I would have to switch to a completely different kind of work, such as doing business or manual labor.

For me, this laptop is not just a piece of technology; it represents my career, independence, and financial stability. That’s why it is something I truly depend on in my daily life.
