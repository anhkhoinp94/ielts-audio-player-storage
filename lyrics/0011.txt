Talk about a book you’ve read and really enjoyed.

I would like to talk about a book I’ve read and really enjoyed, which is How to Win Friends and Influence People. I read this book when I was still a university student.

At that time, I didn’t really have a habit of reading books; I chose it simply because one of my friends was reading it. The main idea of the book is about understanding people and learning how to treat others in a way that builds trust and goodwill. 

What impressed me most was how simple and subtle some behaviors can be, yet I had never noticed them in real life. I used to be quite hot-tempered, and because of that, I often treated people in ways that made them uncomfortable or even upset. This book helped me realize my mistakes and showed me better ways to communicate and behave with others.

After applying what I learned, I started to see positive changes. I received recognition and appreciation from many people, especially from those who used to dislike me. This book truly changed my attitude toward relationships and personal growth.
