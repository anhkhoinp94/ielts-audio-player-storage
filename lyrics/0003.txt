What goals are you working toward at the moment?

I am currently working toward two important goals: graduating and earning an IELTS certificate so I can study abroad for my master’s degree. This goal matters a lot to me because I want to expand my knowledge and open up better career opportunities in the future. 

Every day, I try to study consistently, especially English, even though it is quite challenging for me. The most difficult parts are listening and speaking, because they require constant practice and real-life exposure, which I don’t always have.

Despite these difficulties, I know that improving all four English skills is essential if I want to succeed in my master’s program overseas.

In the near future, I hope to strengthen every skill—listening, speaking, reading, and writing—so that I can feel more confident and fully prepared for academic life abroad. I believe that with steady effort and the right learning strategies, I will be able to reach my goals and create better opportunities for myself.
