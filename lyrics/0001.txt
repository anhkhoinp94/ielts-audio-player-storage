Can you describe your daily routine?

I usually start my day with a short prayer, because it helps me feel calm and grounded before everything else begins. After that, I like to move my body a little, just enough to wake myself up, and then I take care of my personal hygiene.

Breakfast is an important part of my routine, so I always make sure to eat something to fuel my morning. Around midday, I often take a nap, which really helps me recharge and stay focused for the rest of the day.

One habit I try to maintain every day is practicing my English speaking skills. Even though it can be challenging sometimes, it’s something I enjoy and know is important for my improvement. In the evening, I usually spend time studying, because that’s when I can concentrate the most.

Before going to bed, I like to unwind by browsing the web and finishing my day with another quiet prayer. These small routines help me stay balanced and give a sense of structure to my life.
