Describe a historical site you have seen.

I would like to describe a historical site I have seen, which is the Independence Palace on Nam Ky Khoi Nghia Street. I have visited this place many times when I was a student, both in secondary school and at university.

The building is very large and impressive, and it used to be the headquarters of the government in the past. What makes this place special to me is its strong historical significance. When I walk around the palace, I often look at the surroundings and connect what I see with what I learned in history classes.

It helps me imagine the events that happened there about fifty years ago. This site marks an important moment in my country’s history, when Vietnam was completely reunified.

Visiting the Independence Palace always gives me a deep sense of reflection and respect for the past. It is not just a place to take photos, but a meaningful historical landmark that helps me better understand my country’s journey and the value of peace today.

Khi đi dạo quanh dinh, tôi thường quan sát khung cảnh xung quanh và liên hệ những gì mình thấy với những kiến thức đã học trong các tiết học lịch sử.

Điều đó giúp tôi hình dung ra những sự kiện đã diễn ra tại đây cách đây khoảng năm mươi năm. Địa điểm này đánh dấu một thời khắc quan trọng trong lịch sử đất nước tôi, khi Việt Nam được hoàn toàn thống nhất.
