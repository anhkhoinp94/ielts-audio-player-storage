Describe a well-known person you would like to meet someday.

I would like to talk about a well-known person I hope to meet someday, and that person is Rhymastic, a famous Vietnamese rapper. I first discovered him when I listened to his song Love 5, which immediately became one of my favorite songs.

What really attracts me to him is the depth and emotion in his music. His songs are not only catchy, but also meaningful, and I can see parts of myself reflected in his lyrics. If I had the chance to meet him in person, I would love to say thank you and express my respect and admiration for his work.

His music has given me comfort and inspiration at different moments in my life, and meeting him would be very meaningful to me. It would feel like a special opportunity to connect with the artist behind the songs that have touched me emotionally.

For me, meeting Rhymastic wouldn’t just be about meeting a celebrity, but about appreciating someone whose art has had a real impact on my thoughts and feelings.
