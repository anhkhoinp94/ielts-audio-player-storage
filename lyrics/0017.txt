Talk about a souvenir you brought back from a trip.

I would like to talk about a souvenir I brought back from a trip, which is a white T-shirt. I bought it when I traveled to Phan Thiet about a year ago.

I chose this T-shirt because it looked simple but nice, with a small seaside design printed on it. The material is cotton, so it feels very cool and comfortable to wear, especially in hot weather.

I don’t keep it just as a display item; instead, I wear it quite often in my daily life because it’s practical and easy to match with other clothes. Even though the T-shirt is not very special or expensive, it still reminds me of that trip whenever I wear it.

The trip to Phan Thiet was relaxing and enjoyable, and I had a really comfortable time there. For me, this souvenir doesn’t need to be unique or fancy. It represents a simple memory of a pleasant journey and a moment when I could take a break from my busy routine and just enjoy life.
